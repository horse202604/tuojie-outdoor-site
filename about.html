Who we are

A factory built around one product category.

Most outdoor suppliers add bags to a long list. Tuojie went the other way: one 5,000 m² base, one material discipline (laminated PVC and TPU), and one production problem — keeping the inside dry.

The company manufactures under its own TORLIRO brand and produces for OEM and ODM customers: importers, distributor brands and retail labels that need a manufacturing partner rather than a trading intermediary.

OEM / ODM

Custom programs, run like production.

Sampling happens on the same line that will make the bulk order. That removes the usual gap between an approved sample and what arrives in the container.

Brief

Send usage, target volume, market, price target and any reference product. We confirm feasibility in writing and flag anything that will not hold in production.

Construction proposal

Material, coating, seam method, closure type, base reinforcement and packing are specified before a sample is cut.

Sample & approval

A physical sample plus photos. Your sign-off freezes the bill of materials, colour standard and logo placement.

Bulk production

Pilot run, bulk run, in-line inspection and final check against the approved sample. Loading photos and packing list before dispatch.

On request Sampling cost, sample lead time, MOQ and bulk lead time vary by construction and are quoted per project. These figures are not published in the company brochure, so they are not stated on this site.
