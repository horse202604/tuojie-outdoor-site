What we actually control

The bag is only as good as the seam.

Waterproof products fail at the joins. That is where our equipment, our inspection and our engineering time are concentrated.

RF-welded construction

High-frequency welding with voltage and temperature held through the cycle, so the weld face keeps an even wall thickness.

Abrasion & weather resistance

500D to 1000D laminated PVC and TPU face fabrics, four-layer reinforced bases, salt-spray and repeated open-close wear testing.

OEM / ODM in one place

From a sketch, a sample or a reference product to tooling, sampling and bulk production — no hand-offs to a second factory.

Export-ready logistics

Finished goods held in our own warehouse, cartons and labels prepared to your format, loading checked against the packing list.

OEM / ODM

How a custom program runs.

Four stages, one team. You deal with the export desk, and the export desk sits next to the sample room and the line.

Brief & drawing review

Usage, volume, target price, market and reference samples. We answer with construction options and the material call we would make.

Sampling

A physical sample is built on the production line, not in a separate lab, so what you approve is what will be produced.

Approval & pilot run

Sample sign-off fixes the BOM, colour standards and seam spec. A pilot batch confirms the process before bulk materials are ordered.

Bulk & export

Production, final inspection, carton labelling and loading — with photos and a packing list before the container leaves.

Send a brief. Get a straight answer.

Reference product, sketch or existing sample — include target volume and market. The export desk replies within two business days with construction notes and a quotation.
