The critical joint

The weld is where waterproofing is won or lost.

Our RF welding line runs with voltage and temperature controlled through the full cycle. That keeps the welded face at an even wall thickness — no thin spot that passes inspection today and leaks after a season of use.

Where a seam is stitched rather than welded, the seam is engineered for the load path, not just for appearance: reinforcement, seam tape and bar-tack placement are specified per panel.

Process

Seven checkpoints between fabric and carton.

Nothing here is exotic. It is the routine that keeps a waterproof product consistent from the first article to the tenth reorder.

Incoming fabric check

Laminated PVC and TPU are checked for weight, coating adhesion, surface defects and colour against the approved standard before cutting.

Cutting & panel prep

Panels are cut to the pattern, with the grain and coating side controlled. Base panels receive the four-layer reinforcement for abrasion and load.

RF welding

Seams are welded on the high-frequency line under controlled voltage and temperature, then visually and physically checked for complete fusion.

Stitching & assembly

Precision stitching line for webbing, zips, linings and hard-wearing joints. Zips are cycled to check slider travel and seam alignment.

Water immersion test

Finished waterproof models are loaded and immersed. A dry interior after the soak is the pass condition; suspect units are rechecked and rejected.

Salt spray & wear test

Coastal and marine models are salt-spray checked. Closures and sliders go through repeated open-close cycles to expose wear before the customer does.

Final inspection & packing

Count, appearance, labelling and carton condition are checked against the packing list. Photos of the packed goods are kept before the container is loaded.

Buyer questions

What importers ask first.

What is the MOQ?

It depends on construction, material and colour count. MOQ is quoted per project rather than published as a single number — send the SKU or reference and target volume and we will confirm.

Can we order our own colours and logo?

Yes. Colour matching is done against a physical standard, and logo placement is confirmed on the sample. OEM and ODM programs both run through the same sampling process.

Do you supply samples before a bulk order?

Yes. A sample is built on the production line so the approved item reflects real materials and seam construction. Sample cost and timing are confirmed with the quotation.

How is the order packed and shipped?

Cartons and labels are prepared to the customer's format, with a packing list and loading photos. Shipping terms are agreed per order.

Can you produce a size that is not in the catalogue?

Yes. The catalogue is a reference range; custom dimensions follow the same material and seam specification, subject to a construction review.

Need a test protocol before you commit?

Tell us the product, the market and the standard you work to. We will answer with what we can test in-house and what needs a third-party laboratory.
