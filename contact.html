Before you write

Three things that speed up a quote.

The faster we understand the product, the fewer rounds of questions.

Name the reference

A catalogue SKU, a photo or a competitor product. That fixes material, size and construction in one step.

Give a volume range

First order and annual forecast. Both drive material purchasing, MOQ and the price break we can offer.

State the delivery window

If you have a launch date or a container deadline, say so early. Production scheduling is easier before the PO than after.
