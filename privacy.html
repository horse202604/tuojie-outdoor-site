Privacy notice.

What we do with the details you send us, in plain language. This page covers the enquiry form and the small amount of measurement this site does.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Huizhou Tuojie Outdoor Products Co., Ltd. (“Tuojie Outdoor”, “we”), the company behind the TORLIRO brand. For anything on this page, write to sales@tuojieoutdoor.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form:

  • your name and company name;
  • your email address and country;
  • the product system or SKU you are interested in;
  • the estimated quantity, if you choose to give one;
  • your message, and the page you sent it from.

We do not ask for payment details, and we do not ask for special-category information.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry: preparing a quotation, confirming specifications, and discussing samples and delivery. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not sell or rent your details. If we ever want to send product news, we will ask first and you can stop it at any time.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are delivered as email through Brevo, a transactional email service operated in the European Union, and stored in our sales mailbox. The website itself is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Both providers process the data on our instructions and may not use it for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for 24 months. If there has been no correspondence in that period, the record is deleted. Records connected to an active order, or that we are required to keep by law, are held for as long as that obligation lasts.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Traffic is measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which reports aggregate page views without cookies and without profiling individual visitors.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the enquiry data we hold, correct it, or delete it. Write to the address above and we will act on the request within a reasonable time. You can also tell us to stop contacting you at any point.

Changes

If this notice changes, the “last updated” date at the top changes with it.
